# Drafts: Impressum and privacy note for the waiting list

Drafts for the owner to complete and have checked. They are not legal advice. Nothing here is on
the site yet. Text in [square brackets] is for the owner to fill in.

A German site that collects email addresses with a view to selling something needs both texts
before the list opens, linked from the page that holds the form.

- A telephone number is not required as long as a quick way of contact exists; an email address
  that is read counts.

## Privacy note for the waiting list (draft, English)

**Who is responsible.** [Name, address, email as in the Impressum.]

**What is collected.** Only when you fill in the form: your email address, the city you name (if
you name one), and the tag of the link you came by (for example "reddit-hamburg"), if it had one.
The game itself collects nothing about you and sets no cookies.

**What for.** To send you one email when a full version of 3DA is ready, and to see how many
people, in which cities, are interested. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you
give by sending the form.

**Where it is kept.** In a database at Supabase, on servers in [region]. Supabase processes the
data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. [If the contracting company is outside the
EU: name the safeguard, for example standard contractual clauses.]

**How long.** Until the email has been sent, or until [date, for example 12 months after you signed
up], whichever comes first. Then it is deleted.

**Not passed on.** Your address is not sold, not shared and not used for a newsletter.

**Your rights.** You can withdraw your consent at any time and have your entry deleted: write to
[email]. You also have the rights of access, correction, restriction and data portability, and
the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one of your federal state.

Notes for the owner:
- A German version is needed if the page is offered in German.
- Under German practice a sign-up should be confirmed by a link sent to the address (double
  opt-in) before any marketing email goes out. For one announcement email this is the safer
  course too; it needs a way to send that confirmation, which the plain table does not have.
